Public Writing

I’ve organized my public writing into three main sections. My featured work at the top, listed by topic, is the writing that I feel most encapsulates what I most regularly work on. All (or almost all) of my public writing is below that and is listed chronologically. At the bottom is the option to subscribe to my Substack.

Featured Pieces
On Women

Apart from one year teaching middle and high school, I’ve spent my entire adult life in women’s spaces: first at Wellesley, starting in 2019, and now at Independent Women, since 2024. I don’t call myself a feminist, exactly, but I don’t call myself an anti-feminist either: a running theme in my writing is my belief that men and women are meaningfully different, which leads to material differences in how we interact with the world, but that we are equal in dignity and in our capacity to exercise moral agency.

On Education

Like many Americans these days, I’m very concerned about the direction our schools and colleges are going in. I’m particularly focused on how the infiltration of ideology and bureaucracy has increasingly rendered our schools and colleges incapable of producing academically excellent students and good citizens, and has made life difficult for excellent teachers and faculty, too. I write frequently about school choice, teacher empowerment, parental rights, and Title IX reform.
